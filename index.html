Baseline: 2026 actuals (ERP)
Each event starts from its 2026 result. The price lever is the average gross price per bowl, protein included. Produce and the add-on costs are the only costs that move with bowls; staff, truck, transport and setup are fixed per event, and shared overhead is the share allocated to each event in 2026. Margins are before any payout to the partners. With every lever at zero, Primavera reproduces its 2026 margin within €1; Waking Life and Paredes de Coura differ from 2026 only by the new drinks (Jamaica juice), whose sales rate is a placeholder.
